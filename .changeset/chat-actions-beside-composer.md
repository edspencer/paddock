The open chat's actions — fork, promote into a project, rename, archive, delete, mark unread, star and detach from parent — now sit beside the composer instead of in a strip that appeared on hovering a sidebar row: in the gutter to the left of the input box on a wide pane, or in a row under it on a narrow one. They are always visible rather than hover-only, and no longer cover a row's title; sidebar rows carry no action buttons.
