Five buttons beside the composer now move the open chat's transcript: Up and Down step one message at a time, Back and Forward jump between the messages you typed, and End returns to the latest. They sit in the margin to the right of the input box on a wide pane, or share the row under it with the chat actions on a narrow one. Scrolling up while a reply streams now stops the transcript following it until you scroll back to the bottom or press End.
