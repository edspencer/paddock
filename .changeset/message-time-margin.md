Each saved user and assistant message in a chat now shows its time in the transcript's left margin, level with the top of the message and staying in view while a long message scrolls past; messages from an earlier day also show the date. A message still streaming in shows its time once it is saved and the chat reloads. The time appears only when the chat pane is wide enough to fit it beside the transcript.
