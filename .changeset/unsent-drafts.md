Unsent drafts are easier to find again. The fleet strip counts the chats holding a message you typed but never sent in this browser, and lists each as a pill with its opening words that links straight back to it; the project sidebar marks those chats with a pencil. The chat you have open is left out, since its draft is already in front of you. Deleting a chat now also forgets its saved draft.
