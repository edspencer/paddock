Discover is now reachable from the Projects header in the side nav, as a
magnifying-glass button beside the `+`. It previously lived only in the footer
utility stack between Config and the theme toggle — 671px away and grouped with
instance chrome, where an early user could not find it, even though what it does
(bring projects into the list) is the same job as the `+`. The footer link stays;
there are simply two doors onto the same `/discover` route now.
