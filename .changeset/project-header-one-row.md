The project header is one row now: the project's name, then its tabs. The status
pill, domain tags, Overview / Repo / Linked badges, the "updated Nw ago" stamp and
the `⋯` menu are gone, and on desktop the Home / Chat / Files / … tab strip moves
up into the space they left — giving back a whole band of vertical space (79px at
1440×900) to every tab.

Nothing was lost with the menu: its "Edit details" only opened the Settings tab,
which is right there in the strip, and deleting a project lives in Settings'
danger zone. The project's one-line summary moved from under the name to the top
of its Home tab. On phones the tabs stay their own row beneath the header, as
before.
