`phosphor`: a dark-first visual direction built on the semantic token layer.

A deep blue-green ground with real chroma rather than a near-black one; a dusty
rose accent that means *the operator* rather than the product; and a family of
five muted machine signals (errors, caution, completion, detached work,
derivation) sharing one lightness per rung so they read as one instrument.
`*-solid` is lit and its `*-fg` is the deep glass rather than white.

Two typefaces carrying a rule: Source Serif 4 for language, Spline Sans Mono for
machine truth — identifiers, paths, counts, statuses, code.

The transcript is rebuilt as a log. Chat bubbles are gone; consecutive tool calls
collapse into one hairline-ruled record block with a fixed kind column and a
tabular, right-aligned metadata column, so a dozen tool calls read as a table you
can scan rather than a dozen floating cards. Sub-agents are `lineage`-coloured
and their nested logs recurse through the same grammar.

See `docs/DESIGN-phosphor.md`.
